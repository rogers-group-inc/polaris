Topology links

Every link on the Device Map topology is one neutral line until it has something to say: a cable takes the colour and weight of its speed, and a dashed mesh link turns yellow, orange, then red as its signal weakens. Warm hues belong to signal alone. The tooltip writes every value out in words.
