Modal

Modals are for forms and confirmations. For an entity detail view that should coexist with the page, use a slide-over instead. Four shapes cover every dialog in the app: a form, a tabbed dialog, a wizard, and a confirmation stacked over another dialog.

Form: a header with its title and close, a body of form groups, then Cancel and the primary action on the right.

Add Thing

Must be unique across the site.

Tabbed: a .page-tabs strip pinned to the top of the body. Every integration dialog has this shape, with General first and Test Connection at the left of the footer.

Edit FortiGate Integration

A stored secret renders blank on edit and is never sent back.

Wizard: a .stepper above one visible .step-panel. Visited steps stay clickable. Enter and → mean Next until the last step.

Rules
  • Widths: .modal 480 px for a short form, .modal-wide 672 px for tabs and wizards, .modal-large for a form with a table, .modal-xl for a full-bleed editor.
  • Title: "<Action> <Thing>" in title case: Add Thing, Edit FortiGate Integration, New Automation.
  • Footer: the primary action is at the far right, Cancel to its left. A third action sits at the far left (Test Connection, Back). A destructive confirm uses .btn-danger in place of primary.
  • Test Connection names any missing required field in a toast rather than sitting disabled. Saving is never blocked on a passing test.
  • Every dialog gets the panel-lock button beside its ×, restores focus on close and traps Tab, including a stacked one built on its own overlay.
