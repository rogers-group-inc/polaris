Time-series chart

Charts are hand-drawn inline SVG inside a .chart-box, in a section of the asset slide-over. Every chart has the same frame: a header (title, polling-method badge, freshness, range buttons), a stats line, then the plot. This is the response-time chart, the one chart that draws verdicts. Every other chart uses two colours: its series colour and red for a missed poll.

These hex values are fixed constants, not theme tokens. The phone charts and the alert emails draw the same marks, and a mail client has no theme. No data series may ever be red or grey, because red means a missed poll and grey means the upstream was down.

Around the plot
⚠ Last successful update 34m ago
  • The stale banner appears once the stream's last success is older than 3× its polling interval, and on the empty state too. It travels into the chart's screenshot.
  • Stats line: "N samples", then Label: value pairs. Current readings belong in the section's status block, not here.
  • The x axis spans the whole requested window, so empty time stays visible. Ticks land on local clock boundaries, with no HH:MM labels past 4 days.
  • A missed poll dives to the baseline and stays connected to its neighbours. A gap the series reported through is not a miss.
  • Several series (per-core CPU, SD-WAN members) get one HTML legend of toggle chips above the plot, never <text> inside the SVG.
  • Colour the SVG inline (attributes or style), never through a stylesheet class: the screenshot sees no stylesheet.
