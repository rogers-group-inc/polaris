# Cards

Three Material card containers: elevated, filled and outlined.

Use `.card-elevated` for a resting card that should lift off the ground, `.card-filled`
for a grouped block with no shadow, and `.card-outlined` for a card on the ground itself.
All three round to `shape-md` and clip their content.

- **None of them pads its content.** The consumer provides the padding — 16px, as here —
  or fills the card with list items, which bring their own.
- Choose by how far the card should sit off the ground, not for decoration.
